Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page explains what personal data the BIO-OX Protocol collects when you use this assistant, what it's used for, who it's shared with, and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Nuno Andrade, operating under the BIO-OX brand.
Contact: protocolobioox@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Data you provide in the form: name, age, country and email — stored in our database.
  • Your answers to the Oxidative Stress Score questionnaire, and the respiratory complaints you select (e.g. asthma, rhinitis) — stored together with your name and email, so we can follow up appropriately.
  • Messages you write in the free-form chat with the AI assistant — these are not stored. They are sent to our AI provider (see section 4) only to generate that immediate reply, and are not kept afterwards.
  • Technical usage data: which step of the conversation you reached (e.g. started the chat, viewed your result, clicked to message on WhatsApp), so we understand where people have questions.

3. What we use it for

  • Calculating and showing you your Oxidative Stress Score.
  • Generating the assistant's replies, personalized to what you share.
  • Contacting you, if you ask, about consultancy or supplements.
  • Improving how the assistant works.

We do not sell or share your data for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share data with

For the assistant to work, your data passes through these service providers, solely for the technical purpose of making it function:

  • Supabase (database hosting, EU-based server) — stores your name, email, age, country, score and complaints.
  • Anthropic (the AI provider that generates the assistant's replies, based in the US) — receives your conversation messages to generate each reply. Anthropic does not use these conversations to train its models.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

5. Health data

The respiratory complaints you select in the questionnaire and your score are considered a special category of data under GDPR, and are stored together with your name and email for follow-up purposes. Any other health information you freely share in the chat with the assistant is used only to generate that reply and is not stored (see section 2). By checking the consent box before starting the score, you expressly authorize this processing. You can withdraw this consent at any time by requesting deletion of your data (see section 7).

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up on your request, or until you ask us to delete it.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time, ask us by email to:

  • Access the data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Delete your data;
  • Withdraw consent you gave;
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

Just write to protocolobioox@gmail.com. You also have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

8. Cookies

This assistant does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page occasionally. The date at the top shows the latest version.
